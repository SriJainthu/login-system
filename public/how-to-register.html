Registration Guide Instructions
Complete Guide

How to Register

Everything you need to know — from filling your details to showing up on event day.

👤
Step 1
Personal Details
Fill Your Information
1
Go to Registration from the menu or click "Register Now" on the home page.
2
Enter your Name, Register Number, College, Department, Year and other details.
3
Provide your Email Address — an OTP will be sent here to verify your identity.
4
Click Next Step and enter the 6-digit OTP received in your inbox.
5
After OTP verification, you'll be taken to the Event Selection page.
⚠️

Each Register Number and Email can only be used once. Make sure there are no typos before proceeding.

🎯
Step 2
Event Selection
Choose Your Events
✓
Browse all available events. You can select up to the allowed limit (shown on the page).
✓
Events marked 🔴 Full cannot be selected — seats are limited.
✓
For Solo events — just select and confirm. No team code needed.
✓
For Group events — leave the team code blank if you are the Team Leader. See Step 3 if you are a Team Member.
✓
Click Confirm Choice. If a registration fee applies, the payment popup will appear.
👥
Step 3
Group Events
Team Leader vs Team Member
👑
You are the Team Leader
Register first. Leave the Team Code field blank when selecting your group event.

After registration, a unique Team Code will be sent to your email. Share this code with your team members.
👤
You are a Team Member
Register only after your Leader has registered and shared the Team Code with you.

When selecting the group event, enter the Team Code and click Verify before confirming.
💡

Important order: Team Leader must register first → get Team Code by email → share with members → members register using that code. Members who register before the leader cannot join the team.

📱
Step 4
After Registration
Your Digital Pass
✓
A Confirmation Email is sent to your registered email with all event details and team tokens.
✓
Visit My Registration from the menu to view your Digital Pass anytime.
✓
Enter your Register Number → receive OTP → verify → see your Digital Pass with all events listed.
✓
Your pass shows your Team Token for group events. Share this with your members.
✏️
Step 5
Made a Mistake?
Edit Your Registration
✓
Go to My Registration, verify with OTP, and click ✏️ Edit Registration on your Digital Pass.
✓
You can change your Name, Phone, Department, Year, Degree and your selected events.
✓
Register Number, Email and College cannot be changed after registration.
✓
You can edit multiple times until the registration deadline. An updated confirmation email is sent after every change.
⏰

Edits are only allowed before the registration deadline. Once the deadline passes, your registration is final and cannot be changed.

🎉
Step 6
Event Day
What to Bring
📧
Confirmation Email
Show the registration confirmation email sent to your inbox, or access your Digital Pass from the website.
🪪
College ID Card
Carry your college identity card for verification at the event venue.
👥
Team Together
For group events, all team members should arrive together. Individual arrival may disqualify the team.
📌

Your Register Number is your identity at the event. Keep it handy — you'll need it for verification at each event desk.
